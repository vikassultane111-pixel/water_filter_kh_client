How Our Technology Works

We use a rigorous multi-stage purification process to ensure every drop you drink is absolutely safe and healthy.

1

Pre-filtration

Removes visible physical impurities like sand, dirt, and suspended particles.

2

RO Purification

Forces water through a semi-permeable membrane to remove dissolved salts, heavy metals & pesticides.

3

UV + UF Treatment & Mineralization

Deactivates bacteria and viruses. The TDS controller blends purified water to maintain essential natural minerals.

Lab Tested

Our filters are independently tested and certified to remove 99.99% of harmful contaminants.
